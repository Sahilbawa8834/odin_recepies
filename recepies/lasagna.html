How to Make Lasagna Making lasagna can be time-consuming, but the results are well worth the wait. You'll find a detailed ingredient list and step-by-step instructions in the recipe below, but let's go over the basics:

Ingredients required to make lasagna

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef or sausage
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Marinara sauce
  • Eggs
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh basil (optional)
  • Olive oil

Steps by Step Process:-

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add chopped onion and minced garlic, and sauté until softened.
  4. Add ground beef or sausage to the skillet, breaking it up with a spoon. Cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
  5. Stir in marinara sauce, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer for about 10 minutes.
  6. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese, eggs, and a pinch of salt.
  7. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  8. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella cheese, and meat sauce. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used.
  9. Top with remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 15-20 minutes until cheese is bubbly and golden.
  11. Let it cool for a few minutes before slicing. Garnish with fresh basil if desired.

Final product

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish that consists of layers of pasta, meat, cheese, and sauce. It's a hearty and satisfying meal that's perfect for family gatherings or special occasions.

Enjoy your homemade lasagna!
